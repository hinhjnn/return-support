Privacy

Privacy Policy.

Your words and app-awareness settings stay on your iPhone in the App Store release.

Effective 10 November 2026 · App Store version 1.1.1

Information stored on your device

Return stores your Echoes, saved ideas, delivery settings, and the timing and state needed to provide its features. Your optional preferred name stays in the host app for the welcome message. Return also stores opaque app selections supplied by Apple’s Family Controls. This information is stored locally by Return and in local storage shared only among Return’s own app extensions.

iCloud

The App Store release does not offer iCloud Sync. If you used an earlier testing version with optional iCloud Sync, your Echoes and supported delivery preferences may have been stored in the private CloudKit database for your Apple Account. Apple operates that service under its own terms and privacy practices.

Moving from a testing version to a version without sync stops Return’s synchronization; it does not delete previously saved cloud records or your local content. Turning sync off also does not delete existing cloud records. Return does not currently offer a separate “Delete Cloud Data” control. Your preferred name, saved ideas, app selections, permissions, membership and active app sessions are not included in that sync.

Information Return does not collect

The Return iPhone app does not require a Return account and does not include advertising or third-party analytics. Return does not operate a server that receives your Echoes, selected-app choices, or app-use data. Return’s diagnostics use status, counts, timestamps and local references; they are designed to exclude Echo text, raw app-selection tokens and exact protected-app names from diagnostic exports. The optional analytics on this website are separate and described below.

Apple system services

If you grant permission, Return uses Apple features such as Screen Time and Family Controls, Device Activity, notifications, and Live Activities. Apple controls those system services under Apple’s terms and privacy practices. Return receives only the access Apple makes available through those system APIs. Echoes supplied to notifications and Live Activities may be visible on your Lock Screen. If you add a Return widget, its selected Echo may be visible on your Home Screen or Lock Screen. The in-app Home preview may also appear in the app switcher. iOS controls those displays and delivery timing.

Membership and payments

Apple processes membership payments. Return uses StoreKit to verify membership and stores the experience start, entitlement status and expiry needed to apply it. Return does not receive your payment-card or bank-account details through this flow. No paid subscription starts automatically when you begin Return’s initial 72-hour experience.

Support correspondence

If you email return.app.support@gmail.com, you choose what information to send. Google processes that email under its Privacy Policy. Return does not sell information you send for support. Please do not include private Echo text or other sensitive information unless it is necessary for support. Return Support uses correspondence to troubleshoot the issue and reply to you.

Support correspondence is deleted within 90 days after the support case is closed, unless retention is legally required. You may request earlier deletion by emailing Return Support from the original address.

This website

This static site is hosted by GitHub Pages. When you visit it, GitHub may process technical information such as your IP address, browser information, and request logs under the GitHub General Privacy Statement.

Optional website analytics

With your permission, this website uses Google Analytics 4 to understand which pages people visit, where visits come from, and which App Store buttons they select. Google receives page views, public page titles and addresses without query strings or fragments, referring website origins, selected campaign labels, and App Store click events with the originating page and button position. Google also processes technical information such as browser and device information, approximate location and cookie identifiers. Your connection exposes your IP address to Google, which can use it to derive approximate location. See Google’s data collection information and Privacy Policy.

We do not load the Google Analytics tag or send website analytics before you choose “Allow analytics”. You can choose “No thanks” or change your choice through “Analytics settings” in the footer. Refusing analytics does not affect the site or your ability to visit the App Store. Your choice is saved in this browser’s local storage for up to 180 days when storage is available.

If you allow analytics, Google uses first-party cookies such as _ga and _ga_HGEEVW2KSK. We configure these to expire after 180 days without extending that period on each visit. Turning analytics off stops subsequent collection from this site and removes those cookies in this browser; it does not erase events already received by Google. Clearing browser storage also removes your saved choice.

We configure Google Analytics user-level and event-level retention to two months, with retention reset on new activity disabled. This setting does not apply to standard aggregated reports. Google may process information on servers outside your country. Website analytics does not send your Echoes, names, email addresses, or information from the Return app. We do not enable Google signals, advertising personalisation, or automatic form and outbound-link tracking.

Website analytics only starts after you allow it in this browser.

Links to the App Store

Our App Store links include campaign labels identifying this website or the guide you came from. Apple processes visits and downloads under its Privacy Policy and may report aggregated campaign results to us. Clicking an App Store button does not tell us whether you downloaded Return. These campaign labels remain part of the links whether or not you allow website analytics.

Deleting information

Individual Echoes and saved ideas

In the current App Store version (1.1.1), creating, editing and deleting individual saved Echoes and ideas in the app requires an active initial 72-hour experience or membership. Reading saved content remains available afterward. To delete an Echo while that access is active, open its three-dot menu, choose Delete, and confirm. Manage saved ideas from the Home card’s pencil control.

Deleting a saved item is not a promise to erase every copy of it. An existing session or a notification already delivered by iOS may retain its own snapshot until that session or system item is cleared. Backups and any records from an earlier testing version are separate.

Removing Return from your iPhone

You do not need a Return membership to delete the app through iOS. In Settings > General > iPhone Storage > Return, choose Delete App to remove the installed app and its related local data. Offload App keeps its documents and data; removing only the Home Screen icon also leaves the app installed. See Apple’s storage guidance.

Deleting the app does not cancel a membership. Manage or cancel it separately in Settings > your name > Subscriptions > Return. Copies in device or cloud backups may remain under Apple’s backup and retention controls; deleting the app is not a request to erase those copies.

Earlier iCloud testing data and support emails

If you used iCloud Sync in an earlier testing version, turning sync off or deleting the app does not delete the records already stored in your private CloudKit database. The current App Store version does not offer sync or a separate “Delete Cloud Data” control. Return Support cannot remotely erase content stored only on your device or in your private Apple Account storage.

For help understanding these options, or to request deletion of support correspondence, email return.app.support@gmail.com. You do not need a membership to request help or deletion of support emails. The support correspondence retention period is described above.

Changes to this policy

If Return’s data practices change, this page will be updated before the revised practice is represented here. The effective date above identifies the current version.

Contact

For privacy questions or support-data deletion requests, contact Return Support at return.app.support@gmail.com.
