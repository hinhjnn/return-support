A moment to choose

  1. Write a thought you want to remember as an Echo.
  2. Choose the apps where you want to see your words.
  3. When the moment arrives, choose to continue or return to a saved idea.

You can also bring Echoes back during the day or while using selected apps. These delivery moments are optional. Selected-app features need Screen Time permission; notifications are optional, and iOS controls their timing.

Try it with your own words

You have 72 continuous hours after first completing setup, with no automatic subscription.

After that, creating, editing and delivering personal Echoes require an optional auto-renewing membership. Reading saved Echoes and ideas, and basic selected-app protection, stay free. Local prices are shown in the app.
