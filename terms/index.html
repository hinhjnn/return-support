Membership

Return’s initial 72-hour experience begins when setup is first completed. It is not an Apple subscription free trial and starts no paid subscription. A membership is not required to delete the entire app through iOS or request deletion of support correspondence. These actions have different scopes; see Deleting information for local data, existing system snapshots, backups and earlier iCloud testing records.
