How to Read More Instead of Scrolling

By Return’s maker · Reviewed 9 November 2026

To read more instead of scrolling, choose something you want to read, a moment you already have and a place where the next page will be ready. Then decide how you will handle switching to another app. Ebooks and reading on your phone can be part of that plan.

What do you want to read?

Choose something that makes you curious to turn the next page: a novel, comic, essay or familiar book you enjoyed before. Try a sample or a few pages before making it your evening plan.

If you keep avoiding the book beside your bed, try another one. You can return to a demanding book at a different time. The aim is to find something you would choose when the few minutes arrive.

When will you read?

Use an opening that already exists: after dinner, during a commute where reading is practical, or while waiting for laundry to finish.

Two pages or five minutes are possible starting points. Stop there if that is what fits; continue if you want to and have time. You do not need to fill every spare minute.

If that moment consistently finds you too tired, change the time or try an easier kind of break. The low-energy alternatives guide includes audio and rest.

Where will the next page be ready?

Leave a physical book beside your usual seat with a bookmark in place. Keep the light or glasses you need nearby.

For an ebook, find your place ahead of time and download the book if you expect to read without a connection. Put the reading app somewhere convenient on your Home Screen.

If a social-app icon repeatedly pulls you away, move it. On iPhone you can remove an icon from the Home Screen while keeping the app in the App Library. Apple’s Home Screen instructions.

What will you do when you switch apps?

With a physical book, you could leave the phone beyond easy reach or face down if it must stay beside you. With an ebook, keep the reading app open and review which notifications you want to receive.

A Focus can allow the people and apps you need. Apple explains those choices. Reading on a phone still serves a reading goal; the total screen-time number cannot tell that story on its own.

When you catch a switch, return to the page if you still want to read. If you keep opening Instagram without thinking, change that opening route or try an optional prompt there.
