How to Stop Scrolling in Bed

By Return’s maker · Reviewed 9 November 2026

To stop scrolling in bed, finish your practical phone tasks before settling down: set the alarm, choose your audio and check what you actually need tonight. Then give the phone a convenient place to stay and have an easy next option ready. Start with a routine you would repeat on an ordinary evening.

Make a brief closing routine

Think about what brought the phone into bed last time. You might have been setting an alarm, checking a message or choosing something to listen to. Prepare that first purpose before it becomes a longer visit.

  1. Pick a point in your evening. After brushing your teeth or before turning off the main light may work.
  2. Finish the practical checks. Set the alarm, handle the message you need and choose any audio.
  3. Put the phone in its chosen place. Have a book, a familiar magazine or nothing planned for the next few minutes.

Keep the checks short. A long list can become another browsing session. If catching up with friends is part of your evening, you could do it somewhere other than bed and choose an endpoint before beginning.

Choose a charging spot that fits your needs

A place beyond easy reach is one option. Outside the bedroom may suit some situations. Keep the phone accessible if you need it for caregiving, accessibility or an expected call, and check that you can hear your alarm wherever it sits.

If it needs to stay beside you, work on the closing routine or notifications instead. A Focus can allow selected people and apps while silencing others. Review those choices so you remain reachable in the ways you need. Apple’s Focus notification guide.

A Focus changes interruptions. You still choose what happens when you feel like opening an app yourself.

Prepare the next few minutes

Leave your book at the right page. Select the track or episode before lying down, and use an audio app’s sleep timer if it offers one and you want it.

Choose something that fits a low-energy evening. You can read one page and stop, or put the phone down and rest. The low-effort alternatives guide has more options; the reading guide focuses on making the next page ready.

If you pick the phone up again

Notice what you need. You might have remembered a task or wanted contact. Handle that purpose and decide where the phone goes afterward.

If you find yourself in a feed, close it when you notice and choose what to do next. You can also deliberately continue. After a few nights, adjust the inconvenient part: move the charger, shorten the checks or choose easier audio.

If you want a prompt after a chosen interval, a Clock timer is an option. It runs across apps, so treat it as elapsed time from when you started it.

An optional reminder of your evening intention

If you keep forgetting why you wanted to finish, save a sentence while it is clear: “I’d like to finish today here. The feed can wait until tomorrow.” A note beside the charger or in Notes may be enough.
