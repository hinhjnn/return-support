How to Stop Scrolling in Bed

By Return’s maker · Updated 24 November 2026

Set tomorrow’s alarm before you get into bed. Plug your phone in somewhere you can hear it from your pillow. If you need it beside you for calls, leave it on the bedside table and lock the screen as soon as the alarm is set.

How can I stop scrolling in bed if my phone is my alarm?

Try the dresser for one night. Earlier in the evening, set a test alarm for a minute from now, leave the phone on the dresser and listen from your pillow. If you can hear it comfortably, that’s a spot to try at bedtime. Dismiss the test and check your morning alarm. If you depend on the vibration or need to reach the phone quickly, keep your usual bedside spot. Apple’s iPhone alarm and volume guide.

If you share a room or need your phone close for care or accessibility, you can change what happens after you use it. For example, take the call you were waiting for. When you hang up, lock the screen and put the phone back on the table.

Finish the thing you picked up the phone for

One evening, notice the last thing you meant to do before you ended up in a feed. Give that task a recognizable finish. If you were checking tomorrow’s start time, close the calendar once you have the answer. If you wanted a podcast, pick the episode while you brush your teeth, press play and put the phone down. You can keep listening in bed.

Leave something you want beside the bed

If you wanted to read, leave the book open at your place, within reach of the pillow. If you’re too tired to read, turning out the light can be the whole plan. For other evenings, try one of these low-effort alternatives to scrolling or set up a short reading routine.

What if I’m already scrolling?

You can close the app halfway through a video. If you still need to check the alarm, go straight to Clock, check it once and lock the screen. You can try this even if you’ve already spent half an hour scrolling.

When you put the phone down, or over breakfast the next day, write one sentence about why you wanted to stop in Apple Notes, another notes app or on paper. “I stayed up watching clips last night. Tonight I want to hear the end of my audiobook.” Read it the next time you plug in your phone for the night. A paper note under the charging cable works if you’d rather leave the screen off.
