About these guides

I’m the maker of Return, an iPhone app that brings back your own words as personal reminders. These guides include Return where its experience fits, alongside free iPhone methods and other documented options.

Instructions link to Apple’s documentation. Product comparisons identify their sources; documentation-based steps and illustrative demonstrations are labelled. You can use the practical advice without installing Return.
