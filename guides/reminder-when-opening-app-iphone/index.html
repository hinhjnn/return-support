How to Show a Custom Message When You Open an iPhone App

By Return’s maker · Updated 23 November 2026

When you open Instagram, your iPhone can show you a sentence you wrote earlier: “I want to finish my book tonight instead of scrolling in bed.” In Apple’s free Shortcuts app, use App → Is Opened → Show Notification. The notification doesn’t block Instagram. When Return pauses Instagram, its Before Apps screen can show the reason you wrote earlier. Read it, then choose whether to continue.

Set up your reminder in Shortcuts

iOS 27

Create a shortcut with Show Notification and enter your sentence. In that shortcut, choose Edit → Automation and add an App trigger for opening Instagram. To run it without a confirmation each time, follow Apple’s automation settings for iOS 27.

iOS 26

  1. Open Shortcuts → Automation. Tap the add button, or Create Personal Automation if this is your first one.
  2. Choose the App trigger. Pick Instagram (or another app), then select Is Opened, not Is Closed.
  3. Choose Run Immediately if offered. On versions with Ask Before Running, turn it off and confirm so you don’t have to approve each reminder.
  4. Start a blank shortcut if asked, then search the actions for Show Notification. Replace its message text with your sentence. Save the automation.
  5. Test it: go to the Home Screen and open Instagram. Look for your sentence in a Shortcuts notification. Switch to another app and back to Instagram to try it again.

Based on Apple’s iOS 26 guides to personal automations, the App trigger and running without confirmation. Labels can vary. These steps were not tested on a physical iPhone for this guide.

What should the reminder say?

Think of the last time you put the phone down and wished you’d done something else. Use that detail instead of “Use your phone less.” For example:

  • At dinner: “I want to hear the rest of Sam’s story.”
  • On a break: “I came here to see the trip photos my sister sent.”
  • At bedtime: “I left my book open on the good part.”

If the problem is tapping Instagram without noticing, not remembering what to do once you’re there, try changing the cue that makes you open Instagram.

What happens when the app opens?

With Show Notification, your message appears as a system notification. It doesn’t ask for a response. The app can already be on screen because opening it, or switching back to it, is what triggers the message.

What about Show Alert?

Apple’s Show Alert waits for you to respond inside the shortcut. If you cancel, the shortcut stops. That does not lock Instagram or cancel the app opening.

If you don’t see your sentence

  • Nothing happens: run the action in the editor. If the text appears there, check the selected app, Is Opened and the automation’s running mode.
  • You missed the banner: check Notification Center for your sentence. Focus and notification settings can affect how a notification appears.
  • It shows up a second time: switching back to Instagram also counts as opening it. This is not a once-a-day reminder.
