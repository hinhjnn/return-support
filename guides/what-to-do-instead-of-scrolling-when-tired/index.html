What to Do Instead of Scrolling When You Are Tired

By Return’s maker · Reviewed 9 November 2026

When you are tired, choose an alternative to scrolling that asks very little of you. Play a familiar song, sit with a drink, read a page of something easy, or put the phone down and rest. Pick what suits the break you want now.

These are possibilities, not a list to finish. Skip anything that feels like an obligation. Rest can be the whole break.

Choose something that is ready now

Listening to music can turn into twenty minutes of searching for the perfect playlist. Reading a page may become choosing and buying a book. If the preparation is more than you want to do tonight, use something you already have or leave the idea for another time.

You do not have to turn a break into learning, exercise or housework. Familiar audio, a quiet conversation and sitting comfortably are enough to consider when your energy is low.

A few minutes is a reasonable first try. You can keep going, choose something else or finish the break.

Keep useful phone activities in the plan

Your phone can hold a book, play music or connect you with someone. Those may be the activities you want. Go directly to the album, ebook or conversation you chose.

If selecting something keeps turning into a visit to a feed, prepare the choice earlier. A saved album or an open ebook gives you a destination. For reading, see how to have the next page ready. If this happens after getting into bed, try the evening phone routine.

Keep two or three ideas easy to find

Use a short note or a piece of paper near your usual seat. Make each idea concrete: the album you saved, the book beside the sofa, or a few minutes with your tea.

If an idea repeatedly feels like work, replace it. A list is useful when it makes the next choice easier. It does not need to become another collection to manage.

Have an alternative at the moment you reconsider

I make Return. Its What I’d Rather Do feature holds ideas you save for time away from your phone. The purpose is to make an alternative available in the Before Apps choice, after your own reminder has brought you back to your intention.

For example, an Echo could say “I’d like to listen to some music and leave the feed for later.” A saved idea could name the album. When you pause before a selected app, you have something concrete to return to as well as the choice to continue.

Writing an idea in Return does not start the activity or track it as completed. Notes remains enough if you only need somewhere to keep a list. Return is an option when you want that reminder and alternative near the selected-app decision.
