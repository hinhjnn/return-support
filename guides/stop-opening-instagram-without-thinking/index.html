How to Stop Opening Instagram Without Thinking

By Return’s maker · Updated 23 November 2026

If you unlock your iPhone to check the weather but tap Instagram out of habit, try removing Instagram from your Home Screen, not deleting it. It stays in the App Library for the times you actually want to open it, like replying to a friend.

Written by the maker of Return, one of the options discussed in this guide.

In this guide

  1. Change the cue behind the tap
  2. Keep the visits you do want
  3. See your reason before you enter Instagram

Change the cue behind the tap

Think back to the last opening you didn’t mean to make. What happened just before it? Start with whichever of these sounds familiar.

“My thumb knows where the icon is.”

On your Home Screen, touch and hold Instagram. Tap Remove App → Remove from Home Screen, not Delete App. Next time you want Instagram, find it in the App Library. You’ve changed the familiar tap without losing the app. Apple’s illustrated instructions.

“I saw the red number.”

Go to Settings → Notifications → Instagram and turn off Badges. If you still want banners for incoming messages, leave your banner setting alone. Try changing just the cue you noticed. Apple’s notification settings.

“I open it whenever I’m waiting.”

Pick one familiar wait, like standing by the kettle. Next time, put the phone down until the water boils. If you catch yourself searching for Instagram anyway, a free Shortcuts reminder can show your own sentence when it opens. If a banner is too easy to miss, see the choice Return shows before you continue.

Keep the visits you do want

“I’m opening Instagram to ask Maya what time dinner starts.” That visit has a finish line: send the message. “I want to look at photos until my tea is ready” counts too. You don’t need a productive reason to use Instagram.

If you meant to answer a friend and only lost track after answering, moving the icon won’t fix that part. Try setting a timer for the visit instead.

How can I tell if the change helped?

Tomorrow, ask one question: Did I catch an opening I hadn’t chosen? Noticing your thumb reach for the old spot counts, even if you then decide to open Instagram. If you never used the icon in the first place, try the cue that actually brought you back.

See your reason before you enter Instagram

In Return, you can save a line like “I like having my first coffee without the feed.” Select Instagram and allow Screen Time access. When Return pauses the app, its Before Apps screen brings back that line, called an Echo. You can continue into Instagram or put the phone down and finish your coffee.
